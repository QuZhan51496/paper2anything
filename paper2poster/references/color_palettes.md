## Usage Guidelines

- **Primary**: Title bar background, section header backgrounds
- **Secondary**: Section borders, accent elements, sub-headers
- **Accent**: Highlighted keywords, light backgrounds for callout boxes
- **Background**: Poster background (usually white or very light)
- **Text**: Body text color (dark, high contrast with background)

## Accessibility

- Ensure contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 between text and background
- Avoid red-green combinations (colorblind unfriendly)
- Test readability at distance (poster is viewed from ~1 meter)
